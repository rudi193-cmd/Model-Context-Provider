# ask: Willow, its code, comments and commits each answering in turn

Run by `lay.py` over Willow's box. fair-merge ranks each source of the box
on its own, from its index (the code, its comments mirror, its commit messages, its call
graph when one was made) and merges them in turns, so a big source can't
crowd out a small one, and one that found nothing says so.
