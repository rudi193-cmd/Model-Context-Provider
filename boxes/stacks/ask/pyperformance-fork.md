# ask: pyperformance-fork, its code, comments and commits each answering in turn

Run by `lay.py` over pyperformance-fork's box. fair-merge ranks each source of the box
on its own (the code, its comments mirror, its commit messages, its call
graph when one was made) and merges them in turns, so a big source can't
crowd out a small one, and one that found nothing says so.

## how is a benchmark run
- fair-merge -- --box {box} "how is a benchmark run"

## how are two result files compared
- fair-merge -- --box {box} "how are two result files compared"

## how is the virtual environment for benchmarks made
- fair-merge -- --box {box} "how is the virtual environment for benchmarks made"

## where is the list of benchmarks kept
- fair-merge -- --box {box} "where is the list of benchmarks kept"

## how are benchmark dependencies pinned
- fair-merge -- --box {box} "how are benchmark dependencies pinned"
